## The strategy: ask for the cheap version first

Do not open by asking to fly to Sydney. Open by asking for the three things
that cost you nothing, in this order:

1. **Is a virtual or online presentation possible?** Many conferences of
   this kind run a hybrid session, especially for overseas young
   researchers. The brochure does not mention one either way — so it is an
   open question, not a refusal. This is your best outcome by far: the
   credit, the proceedings, no ₹1.3 lakh.
2. **Can a late abstract still be considered**, for the AI sub-track, given
   that you have the results already?
3. **If not this year — can the paper go into the published proceedings, or
   be considered for the next conference?** The brochure states that
   proceedings will be published as a volume. A paper in that volume is
   most of the value, and it does not require a plane.

Only if all three fail does the travel question arise, and by then you will
know whether it is worth ₹1.3 lakh to you.

**One more thing that costs nothing:** the brochure says the conference
"aims to serve as a platform for young scholars, researchers, and students
to engage with senior academics." That sentence is your opening. You are
exactly the person that sentence describes.
